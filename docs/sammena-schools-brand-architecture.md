# Sammena Schools Brand & Website Architecture

## Master Brand
**SAMMENA SCHOOLS**

## Current School
**Sammena Pre & Primary School**

## Future School
**Sammena Secondary School**
Target launch: **2028**

## Brand System
- The existing Pre & Primary crest remains the official institutional seal.
- The digital master brand should use a simplified, responsive treatment centered on SAMMENA SCHOOLS.
- School-level identities inherit the master brand and retain the institutional relationship.
- Sammena blue and gold are the primary digital identity colors; purple is a controlled supporting accent.

## Content Governance
Only publish verified school facts. Clearly distinguish:
- verified facts
- content requiring confirmation
- future / 2028 plans

Never invent facilities, statistics, dates, testimonials, registration information, or school claims.

## Technical Direction
The project already uses Next.js and TypeScript. The website should evolve toward:
- responsive institutional frontend
- CMS-managed content
- admissions workflow
- role-based portals
- future SIS integration
- SEO and accessibility
- CI/CD and production verification
