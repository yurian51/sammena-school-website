# Sammena Website Content Governance

Before any public content is treated as production-ready, classify it as one of:

## VERIFIED
Confirmed from official Sammena material or directly confirmed by authorized school leadership.

## TO CONFIRM
Potentially useful content that requires confirmation before publication.

## FUTURE
Planned initiatives, including the Secondary School target for 2028, that must be clearly presented as future plans.

## Prohibited Without Verification
- Invented enrollment figures
- Invented staff counts
- Unverified facilities
- Unverified awards or achievements
- Unverified testimonials
- Unverified registration numbers
- Unverified fees
- Unverified dates or opening claims
- Claims of partnerships or affiliations that have not been confirmed

## Existing Website Audit Notes
The current homepage contains a number of specific claims and metrics that must be independently verified before they are retained in the new institutional site. This includes founding-year wording, enrollment/statistics, boarding and facilities claims, orphan-support statistics, and testimonial text.

The redesign should therefore treat the existing implementation as a source of UI/content candidates, not as automatic proof that every statement is accurate.
