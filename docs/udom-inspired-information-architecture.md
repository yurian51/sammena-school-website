# SAMMENA SCHOOLS — Institutional Information Architecture

## Design direction

Use the strongest structural patterns of the University of Dodoma website as inspiration, not a visual copy: institutional utility bar, clear global navigation, welcome/institutional message, announcements, news, document/resource centre, measurable institutional highlights, leadership, and a strong footer. UDOM's current public site demonstrates these patterns through its welcome note, latest announcements, latest news, university documents, statistics, leadership and contact areas. The Sammena implementation adapts those ideas for a primary/secondary school context.

## Content governance

Do not publish student counts, staff counts, facilities, accreditation/registration statements, testimonials, academic claims or future-school operational details unless verified by Sammena. Draft content may be stored in CMS but must not be presented as verified public fact.

## Design principle

The result should feel like a serious Tanzanian educational institution with the information architecture discipline of a university website, while remaining warmer, more accessible and more parent-focused than a university portal.
