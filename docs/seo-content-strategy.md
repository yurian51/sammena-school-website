# SAMMENA SCHOOLS SEO & Content Strategy

## Primary intent

Make Sammena easy to discover for parents researching the school, admissions, academics, school life and future secondary education.

## Content pillars

1. School identity and trust
2. Admissions and enrollment
3. Academics and learning
4. School life and student development
5. News, announcements and events
6. Parent resources
7. Secondary School 2028 expansion

## Page requirements

Each indexable page should have a unique title, description, canonical URL, logical heading structure and useful internal links.

## Local discovery

Where official information is verified, publish consistent institution name, location, contact details and school category across the website and approved local profiles.

## Content rules

- Never keyword-stuff.
- Never publish invented rankings, awards, statistics or testimonials.
- Date news and announcements.
- Archive outdated information rather than silently presenting it as current.
- Use descriptive image alt text.
- Keep public content useful to parents first and search engines second.
