# SAMMENA SCHOOLS Design System

## Visual direction

Institutional, confident, warm and modern. Use the UDOM reference for information architecture and institutional density, but do not clone its visual identity.

## Brand palette

- Institutional navy/blue: primary trust color
- Gold: accent, action emphasis and institutional highlights
- Warm neutral: page backgrounds and content separation
- White: cards and readable surfaces

## Typography

Poppins for headings and institutional display; Inter as supporting UI text where appropriate.

## Components

- Utility bar
- Institutional header/navigation
- Hero
- Breadcrumbs
- Section headings
- Announcement strip
- News cards
- Resource cards
- School cards
- Timeline/process steps
- FAQ accordions
- CTA bands
- Footer

## UX principles

1. Every important task should be reachable within a few clicks.
2. Mobile navigation is a first-class experience.
3. Use clear hierarchy rather than excessive decoration.
4. Prefer real content over decorative statistics.
5. CTAs should describe an action: Apply, Contact, Download, View Calendar.
6. Avoid dead-end pages and fake portal states.
7. Preserve visible focus states and keyboard navigation.
8. Respect reduced-motion preferences.

## Quality target

The finished website should feel appropriate for a growing Tanzanian education institution today and remain credible when the Secondary School launches in 2028.
