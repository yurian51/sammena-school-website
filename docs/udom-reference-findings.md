# UDOM Reference Findings

## Source

The reference site reviewed was [The University of Dodoma](https://www.udom.ac.tz/), accessed during the Sammena website redesign review.

## Observed institutional structure

UDOM presents a large institutional homepage with a programme finder as an early action, a welcome note from institutional leadership, an about/background section, latest announcements, news and blog content, institutional documents, staff profiles, partner links, and a detailed footer with contact information.

The page uses an operational university pattern rather than a simple brochure pattern: visitors can discover programmes, read official notices, access documents, read institutional stories, view leadership/staff information, and move toward contact or application-related actions.

## Translation for Sammena

Sammena should adopt the underlying pattern rather than copy UDOM's visual identity. The relevant ideas are a clear institutional utility bar, a strong primary navigation, a programme or school-pathway discovery action, verified announcements, news/events, resource documents, leadership or school identity content, admissions conversion points, and a structured footer with contact and service links.

Sammena should preserve its own navy-and-gold school brand, child-centred language, school-life imagery, and pre-primary/primary admissions context. UDOM-specific university language, staff titles, programme terminology, imagery, and content must not be copied.
